About Us

Faith Reformed Baptist Church is a Reformed Baptist congregation serving the Sunbury and Delaware, Ohio area. This is the church’s official website.

Our mission

The mission of Faith Reformed Baptist Church is to know the Lord Jesus Christ and to make Him known. We do this by proclaiming Christ's Word, by making disciples of Christ, and by serving one another and our neighbors.

What we believe

Faith Reformed Baptist Church is in the process of adopting its constitution. We subscribe to the Second London Baptist Confession of Faith (1689).

Sunday worship

We gather for worship on Sundays at 10:30 a.m. and most often meet at Oxford Township Hall. Check the homepage for the current worship schedule and location.
